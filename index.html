About the resort

A retreat on the edge of the wild

Nestled along National Highway 37, right beside the world-renowned Kaziranga National Park, Grasslands Resort blends nature, relaxation and warm Assamese hospitality.

Our prime location places you moments away from the lush wilderness of this UNESCO World Heritage Site — an ideal base for wildlife enthusiasts, family vacationers, corporate groups and guests celebrating special occasions.

The resort features 21 well-appointed Executive Rooms overlooking landscaped gardens, alongside an elegant Presidential Suite with a separate bedroom and living room. Days unwind at the swimming pool, in the gardens, or over a culinary journey at our multi-cuisine restaurant — local Assamese flavours, Indian classics and international favourites.

From banquet dinners to boardroom meetings and open-air weddings, our team ensures every stay — and every occasion — is comfortable, memorable, and truly close to nature.
